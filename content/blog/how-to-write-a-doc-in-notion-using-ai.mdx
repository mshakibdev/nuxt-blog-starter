# How to Write a Doc in Notion using AI

Notion’s versatile workspace paired with AI tools makes document creation a breeze. This guide walks you through using artificial intelligence to draft, enhance, and organize docs in Notion. Let’s dive in!

## Step 1: Creating Your Page

Launch Notion (via app or [notion.so](https://www.notion.so)):
- Click “New Page” in the sidebar or type `/page` in an existing page and hit Enter.
- Title it by clicking “Untitled” at the top—try “AI-Assisted Notes” or something specific.

## Step 2: Writing with AI Power

AI can fuel your content:
- **Notion AI**: If you have Notion AI enabled, type `/AI` or hit the “AI” button (a wand icon) in a block. Ask it to “Write a 100-word project intro” or “Brainstorm blog ideas.” Results appear right in your doc.
- **External AI**: Use a tool like ChatGPT, request “Draft a meeting agenda,” then paste it into a Notion block.

## Step 3: Refining with AI Tools

Polish your work with AI:
- **Rewrite**: Highlight text, use Notion AI’s “Improve writing” or “Rephrase” option to tweak phrasing.
- **Summarize**: Select a chunk of text and ask Notion AI to “Summarize” for concise takeaways.
- **Tone Adjust**: With Notion AI, change tone (e.g., “Make this formal”) for audience fit.

## Step 4: Structuring and Enhancing

Add structure with AI flair:
- **Headings**: Type `/h1` or `/h2` for headings—ask AI to suggest section titles if stuck.
- **Lists**: Request “List 5 action items” from AI, paste them, and format with `/bullet`:
  - Review budget
  - Assign tasks
- **Tables**: Type `/table`, then use AI to fill it (e.g., “Generate a task tracker”).

## Step 5: Collaborating and Finalizing

Wrap up with AI and teamwork:
- **Share**: Click “Share,” invite collaborators, and let Notion AI suggest edits via comments (type `/AI` in a comment).
- **Export**: Use the three-dot menu to “Export” as PDF or Markdown for sharing.
- **Review**: Ask Notion AI to “Proofread” your doc for a final polish.

## Conclusion

Writing a doc in Notion with AI blends creativity with efficiency. From drafting to refining, AI tools elevate your workflow, making every page sharper and smarter. Start your next Notion doc with AI today!
