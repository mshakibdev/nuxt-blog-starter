# How to Write a Doc in Notion

Notion is more than just a note-taking app—it’s a flexible workspace where you can write, organize, and collaborate on documents with ease. This guide will walk you through creating a standout doc in Notion. Let’s jump in!

## Step 1: Setting Up Your Page

Start by opening Notion (via app or browser at [notion.so](https://www.notion.so)). Here’s how to begin:
- Click “New Page” in the sidebar or type `/page` in an existing page and hit Enter.
- Name your doc by clicking “Untitled” at the top and typing something descriptive, like “Project Plan” or “Meeting Notes.”

## Step 2: Writing Your Content

Notion’s block-based system makes writing intuitive:
- **Text**: Click anywhere and start typing. Each line is a “block” you can move or edit.
- **Headings**: Type `/h1`, `/h2`, or `/h3` and select from the menu to add structure (e.g., Heading 1 for main sections).
- **Lists**: Use `/bullet` or `/num` for:
  - Quick ideas
  - Or numbered steps

## Step 3: Formatting with Flair

Make your doc shine with Notion’s formatting options:
- **Bold & Italics**: Highlight text and use shortcuts (Cmd/Ctrl+B for **bold**, Cmd/Ctrl+I for *italics*) or the pop-up toolbar.
- **Blocks**: Drag the six-dot handle next to any block to rearrange content effortlessly.
- **Callouts**: Type `/callout` for a standout note, like:
  > Tip: Keep it concise!

## Step 4: Adding Media and Structure

Notion excels at integrating extras:
- **Images**: Type `/image`, upload a file, and adjust its size or alignment.
- **Tables**: Use `/table` for a basic grid or `/table inline` for a lightweight option to organize data.
- **Embeds**: Add files or links (e.g., `/pdf` or `/video`) to enrich your doc.

## Step 5: Collaboration and Finalizing

Notion’s built for teamwork:
- **Share**: Click “Share” at the top, invite collaborators, and set permissions (view, comment, edit).
- **Comments**: Highlight text, click the speech bubble, and drop a note for your team.
- **Export**: Ready to share offline? Go to the three-dot menu, select “Export,” and choose PDF or Markdown.

## Conclusion

Writing a doc in Notion combines simplicity with powerful customization. Whether you’re drafting solo or collaborating, these steps will help you create something organized and impactful. Start experimenting today!
